1. **Using Dimts.** You must be at least 18, and you must use Dimts for
   lawful business purposes. Dimts is not for emergency calls: it cannot
   reach 911, 112, 999 or other emergency numbers.
2. **Recording.** You agree that calls through Dimts are recorded, and that
   the other person hears *"This call is recorded and summarized by Dimts
   AI."* Where the law asks for explicit agreement (Germany, Austria and
   the Gulf states), both people press 1 first. You are responsible for using recordings lawfully. That includes
   the law where you live and where the people you call live.
3. **AI notes.** Summaries, names and suggested next steps are generated by
   AI and can be wrong. Check them before you rely on them. Dimts never sends
   an email or books a meeting without your approval.
4. **Data processing.** For the people you speak with, you are the
   controller and Dimts is your processor. Dimts processes their data only
   to provide the service on your instructions; keeps it confidential and
   secure; uses only the listed subprocessors and tells you before adding
   one; helps you answer requests from the people you called; tells you
   without undue delay about a breach affecting your data; and deletes it
   when you delete it or close your account. These terms are the
   data-processing agreement between you and Dimts (GDPR Article 28).
   *Lawyer: confirm whether a separate signed DPA is needed for business customers.*
5. **Your content.** Your recordings and notes belong to you. You allow
   Dimts to process them only to provide the service to you.
6. **Fair use.** Do not use Dimts for harassment, unlawful surveillance,
   spam or fraud. We may suspend accounts that do.
7. **Changes and ending.** You can stop using Dimts and delete your account
   at any time. If these terms change, we will ask you to accept the new
   version in the app.
8. **Liability.** Dimts is provided as is, to the extent the law allows.
   Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer protection law.

9. **Plans and payment.** Paid plans renew automatically every month or year until you cancel. Before you pay, the app shows the price, the renewal interval, the minutes included and how to cancel. Payments are processed by Polar (polar.sh), our merchant of record, which charges any VAT or sales tax and issues your receipts.
10. **Minutes and credits.** Plan minutes reset on the first of each month (UTC) and do not roll over. Calls to or from some countries, and calls after your plan minutes run out, use prepaid credits at the rate shown in the app before the call connects. Credits never expire. If your minutes and credits run out, new calls are not connected until you add more.
11. **Cancelling and refunds.** Cancel any time in the app under Plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and then returns to Free. If you are a consumer in the EU or UK, you can withdraw within 14 days of buying and get a refund, minus the value of minutes and credits you have already used. Unused credits are refunded on request when you delete your account. Nothing here limits your rights under consumer law.

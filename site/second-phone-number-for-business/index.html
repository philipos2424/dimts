Your options for a second number

  • A second SIM or eSIM. Most phones support two lines. You pay a second carrier plan, and nothing is recorded or organised.
  • A second phone. Clear separation, but two devices to charge and carry.
  • A business phone app. A virtual number on your existing phone, usually with a monthly fee. This is where Dimts fits.

What you get with a Dimts business number

  • A local business number on Pro and Business plans, in your own area code where one is free. US and Canadian numbers today; UK and EU numbers are next.
  • Calls to that number ring the phone you already have. No new SIM, no second phone.
  • Your personal number stays private when you call out from Dimts.
  • Every call gets a consent notice, then a list of what was agreed, what you owe and what you're waiting on, and a drafted follow-up.
  • Works on iPhone and Android, and in your browser.

Why the number matters less than what happens after the call

Any provider can give you a number. The money is lost after the call: the extra you agreed and forgot to invoice, the quote you promised by Thursday. Dimts keeps those on each customer's page until they're done. See how it works for contractors and real estate agents.

Questions

Can I have a second phone number for business on my iPhone?

Yes. You can add an eSIM line, or use an app like Dimts that gives you a business number which rings your existing iPhone.

Do I need a second SIM for a business number?

No. A Dimts business number works over the line you already have; calls to it ring your phone like any other call.

Which countries are Dimts business numbers available in?

US and Canadian numbers today, matched to your area code where one is free. UK and EU numbers are next. Calls to and from the US, Canada, the UK and the EEA are covered by plan minutes.

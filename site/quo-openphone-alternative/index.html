A Quo (OpenPhone) alternative built around what was promised

Quo, formerly OpenPhone, is a business phone system for teams: shared numbers, shared inboxes, texting. Dimts is a different tool, for people who sell by phone and need to remember what every customer agreed.

Updated October 2026.

When Quo is the better choice

  • You need shared numbers and a shared inbox for a team.
  • Business texting is central to how you work.
  • You need phone-system features like call routing, menus and team analytics.

Those are Quo's strengths, and Dimts doesn't try to replace them.

When Dimts is the better choice

  • You lose money after calls, not during them. Dimts lists what was agreed, what you owe and what you're waiting on, for every customer, across all your calls with them.
  • You need proof. Every price and date links to the second it was said, so "I never agreed to that" is settled by pressing play.
  • You call across borders. Dimts plays a recording notice on every call, and on Gulf, German and Austrian numbers asks both people to press 1.
  • You want the follow-up written. The email or WhatsApp message confirming the deal is drafted from the call.

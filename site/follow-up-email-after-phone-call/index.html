Follow-up email after a phone call: templates that work

The follow-up email turns a phone call into a record both sides can point to. Send it the same day, keep it short, and repeat exactly what was agreed. Here are seven templates you can copy.

Updated October 2026.

What a good follow-up email includes

  1. Thanks, in one line. No paragraphs about how great the call was.
  2. What was agreed, in their words where possible. Prices, quantities and dates exactly as said.
  3. What you'll do, and by when.
  4. What you're waiting on from them. Polite, specific, with a date.

Send it within a few hours. The longer you wait, the more the two memories of the call drift apart.

Subject lines that get opened

  • Following up on our call: [topic]
  • Your quote: [item], as discussed
  • Confirming [price] for [item]
  • Next steps from today's call
  • [Their name], the details we agreed

1. After a sales call

Subject: Next steps from today's call

Hi [Name],

Thanks for your time today. As discussed, [product/service] would [the problem it solves for them].

I'll send [proposal / pricing / demo link] by [day]. Could you let me know [the one thing you need from them] by [day]?

Best,
[You]

2. Confirming a price agreed on the phone

Subject: Confirming [price] for [item]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the call. Confirming what we agreed: [quantity and item] for [price], [included/excluded extras], held until [date].

I'll [next step] by [day]. If anything above doesn't match your notes, reply and I'll fix it.

Thanks,
[You]

3. After a call where you promised a quote

Subject: Your quote: [job], as discussed

Hi [Name],

Good talking today. I'm putting together the revised quote with [the change you discussed] and will have it to you by [day].

In the meantime, could you send [photos / measurements / access code]?

Thanks,
[You]

4. When they didn't answer

Subject: Tried to reach you about [topic]

Hi [Name],

I tried calling about [topic]. No rush, just reply with a good time and I'll call back, or answer here if that's easier.

Thanks,
[You]

5. Client call recap

Subject: Recap of today's call

Hi [Name],

A quick recap of our call:
- Agreed: [decision 1], [decision 2]
- I'll do: [task] by [date]
- You'll do: [task] by [date]

Let me know if I missed anything.

[You]

6. After a first call with a new lead

Subject: Great to meet you, [Name]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for calling. You mentioned [their situation in one line]. The next step is [visit / call / proposal] on [date].

Here's [one useful link] in the meantime.

[You]

7. After a phone interview

Subject: Thank you: [role] phone interview

Hi [Name],

Thank you for speaking with me today about the [role]. I especially enjoyed hearing about [something specific they said], and I'm excited about [how you'd contribute].

Please let me know if you need anything else from me.

Best regards,
[You]

Questions

How soon should I send a follow-up email after a phone call?

The same day, ideally within a few hours, while both of you remember the details the same way.

What should I write in the subject line of a follow-up email after a call?

Name the call and the topic, for example "Following up on our call: kitchen quote" or "Confirming $4,860 for 40 panels". Specific subjects get opened.

How long should a follow-up email be?

Short: a line of thanks, what was agreed, what you will do and by when, and what you need from them.

How a call becomes a list

No new phone, no SIM, no app on the other side. Dimts sits between you and the person you're calling.

  1. Dimts rings you

    Dial from Dimts, or let customers call your Dimts business number. Your own phone rings first.

  2. They hear a notice

    Before anyone speaks, the other person hears that the call is recorded. Where the law asks for it, both of you press 1.

  3. You just talk

    Prices, quantities, dates. Nobody takes notes, nobody puts anyone on hold.

  4. The list is waiting

    What was agreed, what you owe, what you're waiting on, and the follow-up email, ready to send.

Questions

Is it legal to record my business calls with Dimts?

Every recorded call starts with a spoken notice before anyone talks. Where the law asks for everyone's agreement, such as Gulf, German and Austrian numbers, both people press 1 or the call connects without recording. You are still responsible for the rules where you work; our consent guide covers the main ones.

Do I need a new phone or SIM?

No. Dimts rings the phone you already have, then connects the other person. On a paid plan you also get a business number that customers can call.

Which countries does Dimts work in?

Plan minutes cover calls in the US, Canada, the UK and the European Economic Area. Calls to the Gulf use prepaid credits on top of your plan.

What happens after the free trial?

The trial lasts 21 days and includes 20 minutes of calls. Afterwards every past call stays readable and searchable for free; new recorded calls need Pro or Business.

Who can see my calls?

Only you. Call content is encrypted at rest, and Dimts staff tools show account metadata, not recordings or transcripts. You can export or delete everything from Settings.
